Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

This policy describes how ForceOps Solutions, LLC (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with this website and any applications we publish (“Apps”).

Information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name and email address, and use it only to respond to you.

Unless an App’s store listing or in-app disclosure says otherwise, our Apps do not collect, store or transmit personal information to us.

How we use information

Information you provide is used only to communicate with you and deliver services you request. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties for marketing.

Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address as part of serving the site; see the GitHub and Google privacy statements for details. App distribution platforms such as the Apple App Store may collect information under their own policies.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
